# 1990s likeness review — batch 01

Reviewed September 26, 2026. At the user's request, moved to the 1990s after
2000s batch 01. Reviewed 35 players from the 1990–1999 entry-year pool, prioritized
by career value among unresolved Madden 27 faces without recorded tones.
Five higher-priority candidates without suitable local references remain queued:
Tiki Barber, Richmond Webb, Ricky Watters, Mark Brunell and James Farrior.

## Findings and identity checks

Twelve generic-tone corrections; 23 choices retained. All 35 are now recorded.
No new usable Madden 27 head matches were established. Existing preset-only
entries remain excluded. Head availability was checked against the per-game
catalog, including normalized names, suffixes and asset-name variants.

The catalog's Marvin Harrison Jr. and Jimmy Smith entries belong to newer
players; they must not become the 1996 receiver's or 1992 receiver's head.
Zach Thomas's lookup also carries a newer ThomasZachary asset. Existing era
checks reject these matches, and regression tests now cover the three historical
players in both games. Jason Taylor's catalog preset is tagged 2023, so it is
not accepted as a verified 1997 head. No scan aliases or resolution rules changed.

The local `plpo_ThomasZach.jpg` reference was ambiguous for the 1996 linebacker
and was not used for his recorded choice. Instead, his tone was reviewed using
the named photograph on the [official Hall of Fame page](https://www.profootballhof.com/players/zach-thomas).
Jimmy Smith's reference is the legends `SmithJimmey` spelling, already recognized
by the application's existing legend-name mapping; the regular `SmithJimmy`
portrait was not used for the historical player.

All 35 player/year keys are unique within the reviewed decade's pool. Modern
namesakes remain separated by entry year. Earlier college-qualified corrections
are preserved. Madden 26's existing scan and portrait behavior is unchanged.

## Validation and remaining work

`Likeness1990s.test.ts` covers all recorded tones, rejection of unavailable or
preset-only Madden 27 heads, parsed Madden 26/27 exports, Madden 27 roster
additions, manual generic-face precedence and protection of modern namesakes.
The tests initially reproduced unrecorded choices and incorrect export tones;
all five targeted tests pass after the data changes. The full server suite passed
389/389 with no skips, and server TypeScript checking passed on September 26, 2026.

Coverage, using college-qualified curation as enrichment does: 3,221 pool
players, 23 resolved Madden 27 faces, 35 generic players with recorded tones,
and 3,163 without recorded tones (down from 3,198).

Newly generated classes and roster additions use these choices. Existing
exports and stored appearance snapshots are not rewritten. Exact generic head
shape, hair and in-game appearance remain pending. This is the first batch;
the decade and the automatic likeness-generator feature are not complete.
The user moved to the 1980s after this batch; the remaining 1990s queue is preserved.
