# 1960s likeness review — batch 01

Reviewed September 26, 2026. Scope: 35 prominent players entering pro football
in 1960–1969 without an existing curated tone or a resolved Madden 27 face.
Entry year determines this batch, including AFL and undrafted entries.
All earlier decade corrections and existing 1960s curation are preserved.

## Result

Ten generic-tone mismatches corrected and 25 existing choices retained and
recorded. No additional usable Madden 27 scan mapping was established.
These are coarse game palette choices, not demographic labels or precise
complexion measurements. Exact generic head shape, hair and in-game appearance
remain pending for every player in this batch.

Jimmy Johnson's reference was supplemented by the Hall of Fame's
[1961 UCLA/49ers player profile and color portrait](https://www.profootballhof.com/players/jimmy-johnson),
not the coach with the same name. Willie Brown's identity was checked against
the Hall of Fame's [1963 Grambling entrant](https://www.profootballhof.com/players/willie-brown);
the USC player entering in 1964 is a different person. Jim Marshall's Vikings
article supplies a named playing-era color photo; his
[team biography](https://www.vikings.com/team/front-office-roster/jim-marshall)
confirms the 1960 Ohio State draft entry.

Monochrome references include Jim Otto, Ron Yary, Lance Alworth, Ken Stabler,
Larry Wilson, L.C. Greenwood, Bob Brown, Rayfield Wright, Otis Taylor, Willie
Brown and Fred Biletnikoff. They support broad consistency checks only.
Willie Brown's light default was corrected, while the precise adjacent shade
remains open. Rayfield Wright's existing tone 5 was retained; the monochrome
reference does not justify a confident fine-shade correction. Historical color
balance and lighting also limit shade precision, especially for Mel Renfro,
Charlie Joiner, Roman Gabriel and Willie Wood. No photo was used to assign a
demographic category.

## Identity safeguards

Bob Brown (Nebraska, 1964) uses a college-qualified key. The same-year
Ark-Pine Bluff player and the 1962 Michigan player remain unreviewed and retain
their prior behavior. Willie Brown's key includes 1963 and does not affect the
1964 USC player. Existing tone keys for Bob Hayes, Paul Krause and Tony Dorsett
retain their values.

Modern-name portrait/scan matches for John Williams, Mike Evans, John Johnson,
Cornelius Johnson and other older namesakes were excluded from this batch.
The older John Brown row even links to a modern wide receiver's Wikipedia
image; it stays pending rather than treating that URL as verified evidence.
Deacon Jones and Fred Biletnikoff have historical local portrait filenames with
an extra underscore. Those images were inspected explicitly; a portrait does
not establish that a 3D scan exists.

## Application and validation

Newly generated draft classes and roster additions use these choices.
Verified game faces and manual edits retain their existing priority. Existing
exports and saved roster appearance snapshots are not rewritten.

Likeness1960s.test.ts checks all 35 recorded choices, unresolved Madden 27
heads, parsed Madden 26/27 exports, Madden 27 roster additions, manual-face
precedence, prior corrections, and Bob Brown's same-year college separation
through actual enrichment. Before the data change, it reproduced missing
curation and Fran Tarkenton's exported tone 5 instead of 2.

Validation on September 26, 2026: all five batch tests passed, the full server
suite passed 404/404 with no skips, and server TypeScript checking passed.
Git whitespace checking passed. Actual in-game appearance still needs a visual
check after export.

Coverage after this batch: 4,145 pool players, one resolved Madden 27 face,
110 generic players with recorded tones (75 earlier plus 35 new), and 4,034
without recorded tones. All 435 earlier curated entries were unchanged.
This is one batch, not completion of the 1960s. Next in the requested order:
the 1950s. Remaining prominent candidates include Jim Tyrer, John Hadl, Ron
McDole, Larry Grantham, Maxie Baughan, Lee Roy Jordan, Russ Washington and
Roger Brown. See [work order](README.md).
