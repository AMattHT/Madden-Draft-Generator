# 1940s likeness review — batch 01

Reviewed September 26, 2026. Scope: 25 players with database entry years in
1940–1949, without existing curated tones or resolved Madden 27 faces.
Entry year determines the batch, including undrafted entries. The 495 earlier
curated keys, including 26 existing 1940s choices, are preserved.

## Result

Six generic-tone mismatches corrected and 19 existing choices retained and
recorded. No additional usable Madden 27 scan mapping was established.
These are coarse game palette choices, not demographic labels or precise
complexion measurements. Exact generic head shape, hair and in-game appearance
remain pending for every player in this batch.

The references are monochrome, sepia or tinted historical images. They support
broad appearance checks only; lighting, printing and image treatment prevent
precise shade measurement. The six replacements for the light default are
provisional palette choices, with adjacent shades still open to refinement.
No photo was used to assign a demographic category.

Kenny Washington and Woody Strode use UCLA's captioned 1939 group photograph:
Strode is on the left and Washington on the right. The central player was not
used as either reference. Tank Younger's College Football Hall of Fame photo
is helmeted, with his face visible. Bill Willis uses the Hall of Fame's named
portrait. Emlen Tunnell's local reference has an edited uniform/background;
only the historical face was considered.

The eight Heisman references are the named winner thumbnails in the archive,
not newly verified color photographs. They support retaining a broad light
palette choice; no fine shade, hair or head-shape claim is made. The archive
uses Frank Sinkwich and John Lujack, while the database uses Frankie and Johnny.
Frank Tripucka uses gallery image 3/6 (number 18, left). Hardy Brown uses the
enlarged 1955 49ers Team Issue photo linked from his card index. Charlie
Conerly's Giants portrait is tinted. All retain tone 2 pending game validation.

## Identity and scan safeguards

Bruce Smith's 1942 Minnesota entry is separate from the 1985 Virginia Tech
defensive end. The local legend portrait for the latter was rejected for the
1942 player; the Heisman archive supplies the historical reference. Both
entries keep their own curated tones (2 and 7 respectively).

Modern portrait/scan matches for the older Clay Matthews, Mike Harris,
Johnny Adams, George Johnson, Don Jones, John Sullivan, Fred Davis, Jack
Anderson, Edgar Jones, Fred Evans, Ben Jones and Blair Brown were excluded
from this batch. The 1949 Georgia Tech Clay Matthews remains unreviewed;
a regression check ensures a modern namesake scan is not assigned to him.

The raw catalogs contain George Blanda and Otto Graham entries marked
preset+lookup in both games. These lack usable head bundles under the normal
resolver settings and remain generic. Preset-head opt-in was not enabled.
Other selected players yielded no additional usable Madden 27 scan mapping.

Database entry years are preserved even when they differ from first pro
season or Heisman year: examples include Charlie Conerly (1945), Les Horvath
(1943), Johnny Lujack (1946) and Hardy Brown (1947). No selected player needed
a college-qualified key for a same-year namesake. Existing Marion Motley,
Bobby Mitchell and Tony Dorsett choices are explicitly guarded by tests.

## Application and validation

Newly generated draft classes and roster additions use these choices.
Verified game faces and manual edits retain their existing priority. Existing
exports and saved roster appearance snapshots are not rewritten.

Likeness1940s.test.ts checks all 25 choices, unresolved Madden 27 faces, parsed
Madden 26/27 exports, Madden 27 roster additions, manual-face priority, prior
corrections and namesake isolation through actual enrichment. Before the data
change, the tests reproduced missing curation and Joe Perry's exported tone
2 instead of 6.

Validation: all five targeted checks passed; the full server suite passed
414/414 tests with no skips; server TypeScript checking passed. The data audit
confirmed exactly 25 added keys and all 495 earlier values unchanged.

Live lookup coverage after this batch: 2,763 players, zero resolved Madden 27
faces, 51 recorded generic tones and 2,712 generic tones still unreviewed. This
is one batch, not completion of the 1940s. Next in the requested order: the 1930s.
See [work order](README.md) for outstanding decade queues.
