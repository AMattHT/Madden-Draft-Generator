# 1980s likeness review — batch 01

Reviewed September 26, 2026. At the user's request, moved to the 1980s after
1990s batch 01. Reviewed 35 players from the 1980–1989 entry-year pool, prioritized
by career value among unresolved Madden 27 faces without individually recorded
tones. Existing 1980s curation was excluded from selection and preserved.

## Findings and catalog mapping

Restored John Elway's `ElwayJohn_11420` catalog face in Madden 26 and 27,
scoped to entry year 1983. Both game catalogs contain this roster-backed entry
with portrait PID 5629. The existing by-name map instead selects
`elwayJohn_11251`, a preset-only entry. Madden 26 previously retained that
older ID through its fallback; Madden 27 used a generic face.

The reviewed alias requires the target game's catalog entry and exact entry
year. Manual face overrides retain priority. Evidence is the two per-game
entries in `face-assets-by-game.json` and the `PID_Portrait_Mapping.csv`
row associating John Elway, `plpo_legends_JohnElway_Profile`, PID 5629 and
`ElwayJohn_11420`. This is a catalog-supported mapping, not a claim of a new
in-game visual test; its roster source is not labeled as a scan bundle.

Among the remaining 34 Madden 27 generic players, 10 tone choices changed and
24 were retained. All 35 have recorded fallback tones. The corrections are
Anthony Munoz 2→4, Mike Singletary 7→5, Thurman Thomas 7→6, Marcus Allen 7→6,
Andre Reed 7→6, Hardy Nickerson 2→7, Eric Allen 7→6, Mike Munchak 5→2,
Cornelius Bennett 7→5 and Bill Romanowski 5→2.

Marcus Allen's reference is the legends portrait, not the regular same-name
portrait. The catalog's newer Marcus Allen remains excluded for the 1982
running back by the existing era guard. The [official Hall of Fame biography](https://www.profootballhof.com/players/marcus-allen)
confirms the historical player's 1982 entry year. Hardy Nickerson's 1987 choice
is likewise kept separate from the 2017 namesake. All reviewed player/year keys
were unique in the decade's pool.

## Validation and remaining work

`Likeness1980s.test.ts` covers recorded choices, Elway's per-game asset and
portrait, exact-year rejection, parsed Madden 26/27 exports, Madden 27 roster
additions, manual generic-face precedence, newer namesakes and retained earlier
corrections. Madden 26 custom portrait slots keep their existing export priority.
The initial tests reproduced Elway's older asset selection, missing curation
and incorrect export tones. Five targeted tests pass after correction. The full
server suite passed 394/394 with no skips, and server TypeScript checking passed
on September 26, 2026.

Coverage: 4,179 pool players, 12 resolved Madden 27 faces, 54 generic players
with recorded tones (20 existing plus 34 new), and 4,113 without recorded tones,
down from 4,148. Elway's recorded fallback is counted under resolved faces.

Higher-priority candidates still awaiting suitable reference review this pass:
Steve McMichael, Richard Dent, Neil Smith, Roger Craig, Karl Mecklenburg, Andre Tippett, Leslie O'Neal, Clyde Simmons, Greg Lloyd, Jim Everett, Carnell Lake. Their inferred values were not certified by this batch. Also
avoid the `BrooksJamesTony` portrait for the historical James Brooks; a
substring filename match is insufficient identity evidence.

Newly generated classes and roster additions use these choices. Existing
exports and stored appearance snapshots are not rewritten. Exact generic head
shape, hair and in-game appearance remain pending. This is a first batch, not
a completed decade or completed automatic likeness generator. Preserve the
completed 1970s batch when returning to that decade next.
