# 1950s likeness review — batch 01

Reviewed September 26, 2026. Scope: 25 prominent players with database entry
years in 1950–1959, without existing curated tones or resolved Madden 27 faces.
Entry year determines the batch, including undrafted entries. Existing decade
reviews and earlier 1950s curation are preserved.

## Result

Eight generic-tone mismatches corrected and 17 existing choices retained and
recorded. The Madden 26/27 catalogs were checked for matching surnames and
first-name variants; no additional usable scan mapping was established.
These are coarse game palette choices, not demographic labels or precise
complexion measurements. Exact generic head shape, hair and in-game appearance
remain pending for every player in this batch.

The Joe Fortunato reference is the Bears' named historical color portrait
(fortunato_110917_01.jpg). The local plpo_FortunatoJoe.jpg shows a modern
namesake and was rejected. The Bears article also confirms the 1952 Mississippi
State draft entry, despite his NFL playing career beginning in 1955.

Erich Barnes' Giants action photo obscures his face, so it was insufficient
for palette selection. The named 1968 Topps card #102 linked above supplies
an unobscured color profile; the [Giants obituary](https://www.giants.com/news/giants-mourn-passing-of-former-db-erich-barnes)
and [Browns Legends announcement](https://www.clevelandbrowns.com/news/browns-to-induct-erich-barnes-eddie-johnsoninto-legends-during-alumni-weekend)
support the historical identity. Printing, age and lighting limit fine-shade
precision in the card. Tone 5 is a broad replacement for the light default.

Monochrome references include Johnny Unitas, Jim Brown, Bart Starr, Doug Atkins,
Jim Parker, Harlon Hill, Cookie Gilchrist, Art Powell, John Wooten, Dick Schafrath,
Eddie Meador, Jack Pardee, Frank Ryan, Boyd Dowler and Earl Morrall. They support
broad consistency checks only. Cookie Gilchrist, Art Powell and John Wooten's
light defaults were corrected; their precise adjacent shades remain open.
Pardee's reference is from his later coaching career. Timmy Brown's color
action photo is helmeted; Lenny Moore's historical color portrait also has a
strong color cast. Both choices need confirmation under game lighting.
No photo was used to assign a demographic category.

## Identity safeguards

Jim Brown's Syracuse review is keyed to 1957, leaving the 1956 UCLA and 1966
Nebraska namesakes unreviewed. Joe Fortunato's choice is keyed to 1952.
Cookie Gilchrist retains the database's undrafted entry year of 1954; this pass
does not rewrite historical entry metadata based on the later start of his AFL career.
No selected player needed a college-qualified key for a same-year namesake.

Unrelated modern portrait/scan matches for Sam Baker, Sam Williams, Willie Smith,
Joe Young and the 1953 Boise State Ray Lewis were excluded. The Ray Lewis scan
guard is covered by a regression check. Bobby Mitchell's existing 1958 choice,
Fran Tarkenton's 1961 correction and Tony Dorsett's 1977 correction are preserved.

## Application and validation

Newly generated draft classes and roster additions use these choices.
Verified game faces and manual edits retain their existing priority. Existing
exports and saved roster appearance snapshots are not rewritten.

Likeness1950s.test.ts checks all 25 choices, unresolved Madden 27 faces, parsed
Madden 26/27 exports, Madden 27 roster additions, manual-face priority, prior
corrections and namesake isolation through actual enrichment. Before the data
change, the tests reproduced missing curation and Ray Nitschke's exported tone
7 instead of 2.

Validation on September 26, 2026: all five batch tests passed, the full server
suite passed 409/409 with no skips, and server TypeScript checking passed.
Git whitespace checking passed. Actual in-game appearance still needs a visual
check after export.

Coverage after this batch: 3,679 pool players, one resolved Madden 27 face,
63 generic players with recorded tones (38 earlier plus 25 new), and 3,615
without recorded tones. All 470 earlier curated entries were unchanged.
This is one batch, not completion of the 1950s. Next in the requested order: the 1940s.
